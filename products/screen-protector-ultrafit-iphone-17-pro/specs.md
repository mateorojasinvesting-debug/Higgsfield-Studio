## Key feature — UltraFit Tray installation system

The defining product feature is the **UltraFit Tray** — a polycarbonate alignment tray that makes screen protector installation foolproof:

1. **Place the tray on the phone** — it self-aligns over the screen
2. **Press "Press Here" zone** on the tray top to position the glass
3. **Pull the green "Pull" tab** to release the protector
4. **Result:** zero dust, zero bubbles, perfect alignment in seconds
