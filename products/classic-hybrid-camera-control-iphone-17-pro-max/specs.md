3. **Full-coverage Camera Guard bar** — raised camera module cover spanning the camera area, printed "CAMERA GUARD" on the side. Raised **0.8 mm** above the lenses.

6. **Shock-absorbing Air Guard corners** — reinforced corners for impact.
7. **3X Military Grade Protection** — **11-ft (≈3.3 m) drop protection**.
8. **Precise metallic button covers** — tactile aluminum-feel button covers (shown exploded with mint-green accent).
