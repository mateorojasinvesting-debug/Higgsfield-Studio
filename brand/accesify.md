# Accesify — Brand Brief

## What is Accesify

Accesify is a Colombian curator and reseller of premium tech accessories. We don't manufacture — we select. We import and distribute high-quality accessory lines (primarily ESR-branded iPhone/AirPods/iPad accessories) for the Colombian consumer who values design, build quality, and reliability.

## Positioning statement

> *Premium tech accessories, intelligently selected for the Colombian market.*

We compete on **curation + experience**, not on price. The buyer isn't looking for the cheapest case — they're looking for the one that feels right with their phone, that signals taste, that works perfectly.

## Target customer (initial hypothesis — to validate with data)

- **Age:** 22–40
- **Profile:** Apple ecosystem users (iPhone, AirPods, iPad), Mac-curious, design-aware
- **Income:** middle to upper-middle class (strato 4-6 in Bogotá/Medellín/Cali equivalents)
- **Mindset:** "If I'm spending 5M COP on an iPhone, I'm not putting it in a 30k case"
- **Channels:** active on Instagram and TikTok, buys on Mercado Libre and direct shopify-style stores
- **Pain:** generic accessories on MeLi feel cheap; Apple's official are too expensive and limited

## Differentiator

1. **Pre-selected premium** — every product passes a curation filter (build, design, OEM reputation)
2. **Colombian specificity** — sized for Colombian shipping, support, returns
3. **Visual storytelling** — accessories shown at the quality level Apple/Dyson show theirs

## Brand DO / DON'T

### DO
- Treat each product as the hero — cinematic light, slow motion, premium environments
- Highlight engineering, materials, fit specificity ("only for AirPods Pro 3")
- Show product in context of premium lifestyle (MacBook, leather goods, minimalist desk)
- Use Spanish neutro with Colombian warmth in copy
- End every video with the Accesify outro logo stamp
- Show real product photos as the source (image-to-video), never invent the product

### DON'T
- Don't alter the supplier product, its branding, its specs, or its features
- Don't make claims the product doesn't support
- Don't use cluttered MeLi-style "ZONA HOT 70% OFF" energy
- Don't use AI-generated faces for fake testimonials (use real UGC when needed)
- Don't sacrifice product fidelity for aesthetic effect
- Don't position as a manufacturer — we are a curator

## Brand metrics to optimize

In order of priority:
1. **ROAS** (return on ad spend) per platform
2. **CTR** on cold audience
3. **CVR** on landing page (MeLi or own site)
4. **Video retention** (especially TikTok 6s and 25%)
5. **Cost per add-to-cart**

Every creative is judged against these eventually. Until performance data exists, we use `brain_activity` (Virality Predictor) as proxy.
